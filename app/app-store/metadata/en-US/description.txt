Draw one diagonal in every square. Each number tells you how many lines touch that corner, and the lines can't close a loop. Get it right and the whole board fills with color.

Slant (you might know it as Gokigen Naname) is a quiet little logic puzzle. You work it out from the numbers, one square at a time, until everything settles into place.

What's in it

• 54 boards, from tiny 2×2 warm-ups to 9×9, in three stages: Intro, Developing and Advanced. Every one can be solved step by step, no guessing.
• Four short opening boards show you the rules before the numbered boards start.
• New Game+ opens up after all 54: fresh boards at Easy, Normal or Hard, each with a seed you can come back to or send to a friend.
• Free draw is a blank board up to 16×16 with no numbers. Every closed section fills with color, so you can just doodle.
• Stuck? A hint outlines a diagonal and the numbers or path that force it. Tap again to place it.
• Two modes. Standard colors a section once it's right. Hard colors it when its numbers match, and gives you a lock tool for the diagonals you're sure about.

How it plays

Tap a square to turn or clear its diagonal, or drag to paint a whole line. Undo takes back your last move. Want just one direction? Pick Slash or Backslash in Settings. A keyboard works too.

When you finish

Download saves a picture of your colored board to keep or share. Copy link sends the same puzzle to a friend, who can play it in a browser.

Quiet by design

• Works offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Light or dark, or follow your system. Reduced motion. Enlarge the board and pan around if you want bigger squares.
• One-switch scanning in Settings > Access.
• Your progress stays on your device. Close the app mid-board and pick up right where you left off.
