Draw one diagonal in every square. Each number counts the lines that touch its corner, and the lines may never close a loop. Get it right and the board fills with color.

Slant is a quiet logic puzzle, also known as Gokigen Naname. Work it out from the numbers, square by square, until the whole board settles.

WHAT'S IN IT

• 54 boards, from 2×2 lead-ins to 9×9, in three stages: Intro, Developing and Advanced. Each one can be solved step by step without guessing.
• Four one-idea opening boards teach the rules before the first numbered board.
• New Game+: after all 54, generated boards at Easy, Normal or Hard, each with a seed you can reopen or send to a friend.
• Free draw: a blank board up to 16×16 with no numbers. Every closed section fills with color, so you can just draw.
• A hint for when you are stuck. It outlines a diagonal and the numbers or path that force it; tap again to place it.
• Two modes. Standard colors each section when it is right. Hard colors a section when its numbers match, and adds a lock tool for diagonals you are sure of.

HOW IT PLAYS

Tap a square to turn or clear its diagonal. Drag to paint a line of them. Undo takes back the last move. Choose Slash or Backslash in Settings to draw one direction only. A keyboard works too.

WHEN YOU FINISH

Download makes a picture of the colored board to save or share. Copy link sends the same puzzle to someone else; they can play it in a browser.

QUIET BY DESIGN

• Entirely offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Light and dark themes, or follow the system. Reduced motion. Enlarge the board and pan for bigger squares.
• One-switch scanning in Settings > Access.
• Progress stays on your device. Close the app mid-board and pick up where you left off.
