Slant Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Data collection

Slant does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps contain no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Network use

The apps make no network requests. The game and its fonts are bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection and sends nothing anywhere. The Android app does not even hold the internet permission. A link you tap yourself opens in your browser and leaves the app.

The web version at lukesteuber.com/games/slant/ counts page views with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal information. It also offers optional public scores, which send nothing unless you open Scores and choose to share. The apps have neither.

Sharing and saving pictures

When you choose Download, the app makes a picture of the colored board and opens your device's share sheet. The picture goes only where you send it. On iPhone and iPad, choosing Save Image asks once for permission to add photos to your library; the app never reads your library. Copy link puts a link to the current puzzle on your clipboard. The link names the puzzle only, never your answer.

On-device data

Finished boards, boards in progress, drawings and your settings are stored on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Slant collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
